# Preserve only structured high-position re-entry candidates

The strict indicator, relaxed indicator, trend, 513, limit-continuation, and leader routes all enter the observation pool as a strategy-specific candidate, then converge on the same fresh-quote state machine. This change broadens the preservation path for candidates that previously reached `BUY_CANDIDATE`, but it also preserves an already-extended `PRE_BREAKOUT` candidate as a buy-pending re-entry even when the high-position policy says that no valid high-position structure exists. Source labels remain provenance rather than promotion input; the current structure is recomputed during observation refresh. The limit-continuation route still has a verified-seal admission policy, while the leader route intentionally receives the remainder of the limit-up pool.  
**Watch for:** **confirmed** — a previously confirmed `PRE_BREAKOUT` candidate in `EXTENDED_WATCH` or `UNCERTAIN` can remain `NEAR_ENTRY` indefinitely and qualify for priority re-entry presentation, despite lacking a permitted high-position structure.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

All six ingress routes preserve their own strategy identity, but the strict, relaxed, trend, and 513 routes share `PRE_BREAKOUT` and flow into a single reevaluation path. Limit continuation and leader candidates use their separate strategies and confirmation-round rules, so the high-position retention behavior applies uniformly after a candidate has been evaluated rather than at the route boundary.

The new re-entry preservation branch distinguishes hard risks from temporary gates. Its definition of an intact trend excludes distribution risk, but does not exclude the high-position states that explicitly say the stock is extended without a valid second-wave or consolidation structure.

Source and structure are separate in the active path. The route stamps a provenance label on admission, while `buildObservationSignal` recomputes `structureQuality`, high-position phase, and entry conditions from current quote and history; the visible strategy label is therefore not being used as evidence of a current setup.

## Extended high positions remain priority re-entry candidates

**High severity, confirmed.** `ObservationPool.evaluate` turns every non-`NONE` high-position phase except an allowed confirmed second wave into a `trendGate` at [`ObservationPool.kt:110-112`](../app/src/main/java/com/example/stockmonitor/analysis/ObservationPool.kt#L110-L112). `EXTENDED_WATCH` and `UNCERTAIN` therefore prevent a new promotion, but neither is included in `nearStructureGate` ([`ObservationPool.kt:155-164`](../app/src/main/java/com/example/stockmonitor/analysis/ObservationPool.kt#L155-L164)) or in the conditions that make `trendIntact` false ([`ObservationPool.kt:150-155`](../app/src/main/java/com/example/stockmonitor/analysis/ObservationPool.kt#L150-L155)).

For any candidate whose historical maximum stage is `BUY_CANDIDATE`, the new `promotionBlocked && previouslyConfirmed && trendIntact` branch selects `NEAR_ENTRY` ([`ObservationPool.kt:200-202`](../app/src/main/java/com/example/stockmonitor/analysis/ObservationPool.kt#L200-L202)). The three-day weakness and ten-day unconfirmed expiry paths are explicitly limited to candidates that were never confirmed ([`ObservationPool.kt:185-188`](../app/src/main/java/com/example/stockmonitor/analysis/ObservationPool.kt#L185-L188)). A stock that is merely extended or whose high-position evidence is invalid can consequently remain a `PRE_BREAKOUT` buy-pending candidate without a time or structural exit.

The problem becomes user-visible rather than merely historical state. If its recomputed `structureQuality` is non-empty, `ObservationEligibility.isPriorityReentry` marks that `NEAR_ENTRY` candidate as priority re-entry ([`ObservationEligibility.kt:169-174`](../app/src/main/java/com/example/stockmonitor/analysis/ObservationEligibility.kt#L169-L174)). A prior M20 or breakout structure can supply that non-empty quality while `HighPositionPolicy` returns `EXTENDED_WATCH`, whose contract says the high-position consolidation quality is insufficient. This gives an extended name the elevated re-entry treatment that should be reserved for a still-valid pullback, consolidation, or second-wave structure.

Make invalid high-position states explicit structural invalidators for the preservation path. For example, have `trendIntact` reject `EXTENDED_WATCH` and `UNCERTAIN`, or route those phases directly to `OBSERVING`; then also make `isPriorityReentry` reject the same states through persisted structure/risk information. Preserve the existing `CONSOLIDATION` and `SECOND_WAVE_CANDIDATE` handling, since those are named structures that the policy intentionally keeps under observation.

## The route coverage does not exercise high-position preservation

**Low severity, confirmed.** `HighPositionPolicyTest` verifies the policy’s standalone classification and `ObservationPoolTest` verifies major-down and bottoming gates, but neither composes an `EXTENDED_WATCH` or `UNCERTAIN` decision with a candidate whose `highestStage` is `BUY_CANDIDATE`. `ObservationPoolStrategyTest` covers normal pre-breakout and leader promotion, and `LimitPromotionPolicyTest` covers seal quality and opening confirmation; neither exercises the shared preservation branch with a high-position decision.

Add parameterized state-machine coverage that starts with previously confirmed candidates from strict, relaxed, trend, and 513 ingress, then supplies fresh signals classified as `EXTENDED_WATCH` and `UNCERTAIN`. Add leader and continuation cases where applicable to confirm the shared branch does not elevate an invalid high-position state. The assertions should require `OBSERVING` or a dedicated risk/deferred state and require `ObservationEligibility.isPriorityReentry` to return false.

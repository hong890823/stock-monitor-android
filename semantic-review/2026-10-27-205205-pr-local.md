# Candidate-pool deferred participation and risk observation

The candidate pool now separates temporary execution deferrals from structural or market risk observations without changing the buy-stage model. A sealed limit-up or an explicit deferral is kept in the deferred group, while `BLOCKED` market risk, trend weakening, breakout risk, shrink-acceleration, high-position distribution, and a downward major trend are routed to risk observation. The display places deferred participation above risk observation, and confirmed-buy notifications must pass the shared `ObservationEligibility.isDeferredOrRisk` gate through `ObservationBuyAlert.canNotify`. The pending-confirmation overview uses one compass icon for the parent and distinct star/hourglass icons for its two child groups. Watch for: no blocking behavioral gap found in the scoped rules (**confirmed**).

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The classification uses persistent candidate fields for market, breakout, and limit-up risks, plus persisted evidence or reasons for trend and high-position conditions. When a candidate matches both categories, risk wins, so it cannot be promoted by the higher deferred display position.

All confirmed-buy delivery paths delegate eligibility to `ObservationBuyAlert.canNotify`, including real-time pool refreshes, watchlist refreshes, and the tail-session review. The UI presents deferred before risk and keeps the parent pending-confirmation icon separate from the icons on its two subgroups.

## Deferred and risk routes preserve the intended precedence

`ObservationEligibility` identifies temporary deferrals from a limit-up block, a sealing reason, or an explicit “暂缓” reason. It identifies risk from `ObservationMarketRisk.BLOCKED`, a persisted breakout-risk decision, trend-weak evidence, limit-up shrink acceleration, and persisted risk reasons including high-position distribution and a downward major trend. `prioritizedSections` removes risk candidates from the deferred bucket before constructing the risk bucket, so an item carrying both signals appears only under risk observation. The status-group order in `ScreenFragment` renders deferred participation before risk observation.

`ObservationPool.evaluate` persists the risk inputs that require more than a stage check: `marketRisk`, `breakoutRisk`, `limitUpShrinkAcceleration`, evidence, and the stage reason. The signal builder records `当前趋势走弱` in evidence, while the high-position and major-trend paths write `高位派发` and `趋势向下` into the persisted reason. Those forms are therefore visible to the shared classifier after the current evaluation ends.

## Buy notifications cannot bypass temporary deferral or risk

`ObservationBuyAlert.canNotify` requires `BUY_CANDIDATE` and rejects both a limit-up deferral and `isDeferredOrRisk`. Both real-time refresh paths check this method before sending a confirmed-buy notification, and the tail-session candidate filter applies the same method before its independent trading guard. This keeps deferred and risk candidates out of notification delivery even if an earlier state was eligible.

## Pending-confirmation labels use distinct hierarchy icons

The visible overview uses `🧭` for the pending-confirmation parent, `⭐` for priority re-entry, and `⏳` for other pending confirmation. The matching section banners keep the child icons distinct. The static UI test verifies the text and the display order from buy through deferred and risk.

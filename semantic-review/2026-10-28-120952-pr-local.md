# Daily-chart-gated observation promotion and demotion

The change adds explicit high-position phases, lets a confirmed second wave bypass ordinary entry confirmation, and records a candidate’s highest prior stage to preserve re-entry opportunities. The live observation path derives high-position evidence from the current quote plus historical daily bars, then feeds the resulting phase into `ObservationPool`. That integration has two incompatible definitions of the base: production high-position detection requires a 60-day run-up, while consolidation is measured across the same 60-day range; a direct breakout bypasses the consolidation check entirely. **Watch for:** **confirmed** a high-range breakout can be promoted as `SECOND_WAVE_CONFIRMED` without any daily-chart second-wave base; **confirmed** a new quote date demotes every prior buy candidate before a failure is established; **likely** repeated intraday leader-risk samples can demote an already-promoted candidate to observing without daily confirmation.
**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The proposed second-wave path is intended to be a high-quality exception for high-position stocks. Its classifier, however, returns on price and volume breakout before evaluating the higher-low, contracted-pullback base. `ObservationPool` then treats that phase as an independently actionable entry, including for a `PRE_BREAKOUT` route that otherwise does not require repeated intraday confirmations.

The daily inputs cannot normally produce the intended consolidation phase in production. The same 60-day window must both demonstrate at least a 20% run-up to establish `PriceLocation.HIGH` and fit within the narrow range required for consolidation. This leaves ordinary high-position bases blocked as `EXTENDED_WATCH` while unbased new-high moves take the direct second-wave route.

The highest-stage record preserves historical context, but `ObservationEligibility` lowers a `BUY_CANDIDATE` to `NEAR_ENTRY` whenever a quote from a new trading day arrives. That write happens before asynchronous history retrieval and state-machine evaluation, so an ordinary fetch failure leaves a healthy previously promoted candidate downgraded without a confirmed failure.

The state machine gives a first `BreakoutRiskDecision` a bounded re-evaluation path. Leader `riskFlag` is processed before that protection and is set from live drawdown and intraday volume conditions, so a second scheduled sample can move the same candidate to `OBSERVING` even though the daily failure predicates have not been met.

## A first high-range breakout becomes a second-wave entry

Confirmed, high severity: `HighPositionPolicy.kt:75-94` calculates `breakout` from only the current price above the prior range high, current volume ratio, intraday dual-timeframe permission, and the trend authorization. It immediately returns `SECOND_WAVE_CONFIRMED`; the contracted-pullback, higher-low, and stable-range checks do not occur until `HighPositionPolicy.kt:119-147`.

`MonitorService.kt:3259-3283` supplies the prior 60-session high, current live quote, and live volume to this decision. The current session is also inserted into `recentLows`, `recentHighs`, and `recentCloses`, but those inputs are unreachable on the successful-breakout path. A stock that makes its first qualifying high-range push therefore receives a second-wave label despite no completed daily consolidation, platform, or higher-low sequence.

The label is actionable rather than descriptive. `ObservationPool.kt:130-141` converts it into both `entryConfirmed` and `entryCanBuy`, and `ObservationPool.kt:169-209` promotes it immediately. A `PRE_BREAKOUT` candidate has no leader confirmation-round requirement, so this route can reach `BUY_CANDIDATE` directly. Require `dailyBaseConfirmed` before the breakout may return `SECOND_WAVE_CONFIRMED`, and derive it from completed daily bars only. The current quote may test a pre-established base upper bound and execution timing, but it must not create the base it is said to confirm.

The regression matrix needs a production-shaped case with `priceLocation = HIGH`, a prior 60-day high, a rising/volume-confirmed first breakout, and no completed tight base. It must produce `EXTENDED_WATCH` or `BREAKOUT_WATCH`, never `SECOND_WAVE_CONFIRMED` or a buy candidate. A second case should require the completed daily base, then verify only a later quote break above that base may authorize the second-wave exception.

## The 60-day high-position range cannot also represent a narrow base

Confirmed, high severity: production passes `priceLocation` to `HighPositionPolicy` (`MonitorService.kt:3259-3272`), so the policy uses `PricePosition.HIGH` rather than its old 95%-of-range fallback (`HighPositionPolicy.kt:64-65`). `PricePosition.kt:55-58` defines that state as a price in the upper third with at least a 20% gain from the first close in the 60-day window.

For every such production input, `rangeHigh >= price` and `rangeLow <= baseClose`; therefore `(rangeHigh - rangeLow) / rangeLow` is at least 20%. `HighPositionPolicy.kt:119` accepts consolidation only when that same 60-day range is no wider than `max(5%, atrPct * 3)`. Normal ATR values make this impossible. The intended `CONSOLIDATION` and `SECOND_WAVE_CANDIDATE` phases are consequently unavailable after the run-up that makes a stock high-position, leaving either `EXTENDED_WATCH` or the unchecked direct-breakout branch above.

Replace the single `rangeHigh`/`rangeLow` contract with separate evidence: retain a prior 60-day range for high-position and breakout-context detection, and add a short completed-session base window for width, pullback volume, highs, lows, and its upper trigger. Compute the daily base before live-bar injection. The test helper in `HighPositionPolicyTest.kt:28-62` omits `priceLocation` and uses a 3.4% range, so it only covers the legacy fallback; add cases built from `PricePosition.of` data with a 20% advance followed by a tight five-to-ten-session base.

## Quote freshness currently rewrites the protected stage

Confirmed, high severity: `ObservationEligibility.kt:86-103` defines `reconfirmationRequired` as true whenever the quote date differs from the candidate’s last evaluation date. For any `BUY_CANDIDATE`, `applyQuoteGate` clears its confirmation state and writes `NEAR_ENTRY`, even if the quote is open rather than limit-up and contains no trend, structure, volume, or price failure. `MonitorService.kt:2745-2753` persists that guarded list before `refreshObservationPool` retrieves historical bars and calls `ObservationPool.evaluate`.

If the history request is unavailable, insufficient, or fails, `MonitorService.kt:2797-2805` returns no evaluated replacement. The quote-date downgrade remains in storage and in the UI. This is not merely a display freshness marker. It removes the actionable state from a candidate whose previous promotion has not been invalidated. `ObservationEligibilityTest.kt:60-71` expressly codifies this behavior for every strategy.

Keep the existing `BUY_CANDIDATE` stage through a quote-date transition. Let the normal refresh evaluate a current signal and lower the stage only for defined invalidation, such as the current risk gates or a confirmed daily failure. If stale-date visibility is required, calculate it at display/notification time from `lastEvaluatedDate` and suppress execution or mark it awaiting refresh without changing the persistent state. Replace the existing test with cases proving a healthy next-day quote and a failed history fetch preserve the stage, while a returned confirmed failure downgrades it.

## Leader risk needs a confirmation boundary

Likely, medium severity: `ObservationPool.kt:186-194` processes `riskFlag` before the `previouslyConfirmed` and `trendIntact` protection. A promoted candidate becomes `NEAR_ENTRY` on the first `riskFlag`; the next evaluation finds it is no longer currently `BUY_CANDIDATE` and sends it directly to `OBSERVING`.

For leader routes, `MonitorService.kt:3467-3470` assigns that flag from live drawdown thresholds, live volume with less than 1% price progress, or a -1% quote after recent limit-up days. Those are valid warnings, but the path does not require a completed daily bar or the two-round soft-risk confirmation that `BreakoutRiskDecision` receives. A persistent intraday fluctuation can therefore bypass the stated protection for previously promoted candidates before a daily-chart failure is confirmed.

Split the signal into a transient leader warning and a confirmed leader failure. Route the warning through a bounded counter equivalent to `softRiskRounds`, keeping a previously promoted and trend-intact candidate in `NEAR_ENTRY`; use `OBSERVING` only when the daily failure predicate or the configured repeated-risk threshold is met. Add a state-sequence test with a prior `BUY_CANDIDATE`, two same-day warning samples, and then a completed-bar failure to make the promotion-protection boundary explicit.
